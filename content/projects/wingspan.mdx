## The problem

Wingspan is a student-run undergraduate research journal at the University of North Texas. Their website had two jobs: introduce the journal to new readers, and give students a clear way to submit their research, while looking as credible as the work they publish.

## What I did

I designed the site in Figma, then built it in Framer. It came down to three pages:

- **Home:** what Wingspan is and what it publishes
- **Submit:** how to send in a paper, with guidelines the team can edit themselves
- **Members:** the people behind the journal

Making the guidelines editable mattered, because submission rules change and the team shouldn't need me every time they do.

## What I learned

**This was my first paid project**. I expected the work to be mostly design and building, but a lot of it turned out to be communication: understanding what the client actually needed, explaining my decisions, and keeping them updated along the way.
