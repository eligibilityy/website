## Why I built it

I failed my Coprog1 midterms, and after that I couldn't stop worrying about my GWA. So I built something to check it: a calculator for DLSL computer science students that works out your grades from your scores and how much each requirement is worth. For now it supports the current BSCS curriculum.

## What I learned

It's been my main way of getting better at React, Next.js and TypeScript, and building something I actually needed made those lessons stick.

## What's next

- Improving the rounding logic
- Finding a way to import grades instead of typing them in
- More courses, if people start using it regularly
